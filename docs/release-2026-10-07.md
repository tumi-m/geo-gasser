# Walk the streets, a fairer map, and six rounds of fixes

## Street View: walk around the place

Photo rounds can now open in Google Street View, the way GeoGuessr plays:
- **Moving around:** drag to look, and click the arrows or the road to walk.
  **Back to start** returns you to where you began.
- **Starting point:** the nearest outdoor panorama within about 1.5 km of the
  place. Every player in a duel starts on the same panorama, facing the same
  way. That includes online guests, who get it from the scene they already
  receive, so nothing new is sent between players.
- **Nothing gives the answer away:** road names, the address card and the
  date control are off. Google's credit stays visible but its links don't
  open.
- **Switching:** a **Street View / Photo** switch flips between the two.
  Places with no coverage use the photo automatically.
- **Arrival:** the panorama fades in with a "Finding the street…" note while
  it loads.

**Turning it on:** add a Maps JavaScript API key as `VITE_GOOGLE_MAPS_KEY` in
Vercel (steps in `docs/env.template.md`). Without a key the game plays
exactly as before.

## A fairer guessing map

- **No towns while you guess.** In a country with one or two dots, the dot
  was the obvious place to pin. The guessing map now shows only countries,
  provinces and their names. Towns come back on the result map, where they
  help you see where the answer was.
- **No orbit lines in the space round.** The ruler's orbit lines sat exactly
  on the planets and their moons, so they also wait for the result. The
  broad belts and clouds stay, like country outlines.
- **Escape deals real photos only.** The AI-made reconstruction plates are
  meant for the final round of the long matches. Escape could deal them as
  ordinary stops (a lighthouse reconstruction turned up in testing); it now
  never does.

## Fixes and polish

- **Phones:**
  - **Settings is on screen again.** The match header overflowed every phone
    width: on a 390 px phone the Settings button sat off the right edge and
    couldn't be opened mid-round.
    - The round markers now share a capped width. A 10-round Odyssey alone
      used to take 236 px.
    - On 380 px phones and smaller, the music button steps aside; your music
      is in Settings, Sound.
  - **Text fits.** "How to play" stays on one line on 360 px phones, and the
    map prompt now reads "Tap the map to guess" so it fits beside Lock guess.
  - **Landscape hint.** On a sideways phone, "Drag to look around" sits over
    the photo instead of under the map.
- **Results tell the truth:**
  - "Total distance" adds up your real map guesses only. A missed pin used to
    count as 20,000 km, and space rounds are left out.
  - "Best question" shows "—" when nothing scored, instead of 0.
  - The per-question list names both players. Pass-and-play used to say
    "you".
  - A place named after its town no longer repeats it ("Clarens · Clarens");
    the row shows the country instead.
- **How to play** now explains Street View, the town-free map and the space
  rounds in Voyage and Odyssey.

## Checks

- 220 tests pass. App and worker type checks, lint and the production build
  are clean.
- **Full matches** played through to results on a phone: solo, against Grok,
  and pass-and-play. An online duel between two browsers also reached its
  first round with both players on the same panorama. Splash, result-card
  and final scores agree once their count-ups finish.
- **Street View** was tested against a stand-in for Google's API, because no
  real key is available here:
  - walking, Back to start and the Street View / Photo switch work;
  - Google's links don't open;
  - places with no coverage fall back to the photo;
  - in an online duel, host and guest get the same panorama and heading.

  The real panorama look is still unchecked until a key is added.
- **Screens:** audited at 360, 390, 414 and 1,280 px and on a sideways phone.
  The accessibility scan (axe) is clean on home, intro, Street View, photo
  and reveal.
