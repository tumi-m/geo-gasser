# Real places only: a fixed final round, unfit photos pulled, 30 Street View places

## The final round shows the actual place

The last round of Classic, Voyage and Odyssey was dealt from 10
"reconstruction" places drawn with only 6 generic pictures. One Cape Town
picture, with Table Mountain, stood in for Cape Agulhas, Kirstenbosch and the
Cango Caves, 400 km away. The "Cape" picture stood in for the Three Rondavels
in Mpumalanga. So the picture often showed somewhere else entirely.

While the 3D reconstruction renderer is parked, the final round is now real
places:
- **What you see:** a real photo of the place itself, or Street View of it
  when a key is set.
- **Country mix:** the same as the rest of the match, 5 + 5 on SA × NL.
- **Label:** the round is marked **Final round** on its intro card and in the
  header.
- **Reconstruction plates** are no longer dealt anywhere. The parked 3D round
  and its files stay as they were, behind `ROUND4_3D_LIVE`.

## Unfit photos pulled

Every photo in the game was checked by eye. 25 couldn't fairly be played:

- **Not the place (8):**
  - Edinburgh Castle: a seagull
  - Hluhluwe: two hornbills
  - Mahikeng: a stone plaque
  - Prague Castle: a guard in a sentry box
  - Assen: a black-and-white shot of grass
  - Addo: an elephant close-up
  - Serengeti: a gazelle
  - Uluru: a close-up of rock
- **AI-generated "cinematic reconstructions" (17):**
  - **The places:** Boulders Beach, Chapman's Peak, Moses Mabhida Stadium,
    Pietermaritzburg City Hall, Hillbrow Tower, Voortrekker Monument,
    Vilakazi Street, God's Window, Magere Brug, Alkmaar Waag, Volendam,
    Gouda, Breda's Grote Kerk, John Frost Bridge, Koppelpoort, Muiderslot
    and the Oldehove.
  - **Why they had to go:** they were dealt as if they were photographs, and
    they show invented buildings. "Alkmaar Waag" looks nothing like the
    Waag, and "Vilakazi Street" has a made-up "Mandela House" sign.

These 25 are never dealt as photos. With a Street View key they play in
Street View. The photo-fetch script can replace their photos where Commons is
reachable:

```
node --experimental-strip-types scripts/fetch-new-photos.mjs --unfit
```

A place re-enters the photo pool automatically once a real photo is fetched.
The script now also rejects photos titled as birds, animals, plaques or
guards.

Without a key, SA × NL has 69 real photos. Classic and Voyage are unchanged.
Odyssey shrinks to 9 rounds (89 questions) until the key is added; the space
rounds are still whole rounds.

## 30 Street View places

Thirty street-level places, played only in Google Street View
(`src/lib/game/street-locations.ts`):

- **South Africa:**
  - Long Street and Sea Point Promenade, Cape Town
  - Church Square, Pretoria
  - Maboneng, Johannesburg
  - Florida Road, Durban
  - Graaff-Reinet
  - Plettenberg Bay
  - Oudtshoorn
  - Bethlehem
  - Tzaneen
- **Netherlands:** Arnhem, Den Helder, Roermond, Hilversum, Emmen, Zandvoort,
  Urk, Lelystad, Goes, Sneek.
- **The world:**
  - Shibuya Crossing, Tokyo
  - Michigan Avenue, Chicago
  - Caminito, Buenos Aires
  - Queenstown waterfront
  - Ribeira, Porto
  - Tha Phae Gate, Chiang Mai
  - Zócalo, Mexico City
  - İstiklal Avenue, Istanbul
  - Flinders Street, Melbourne
  - Avenida Paulista, São Paulo

**When they're dealt:** only when `VITE_GOOGLE_MAPS_KEY` is set. Without a
key the game never offers them.

**How they play:**
- They open straight in Street View, with no Photo switch.
- They search out to 5 km for a panorama.
- If none loads, the round says so plainly instead of showing a broken
  picture.
- Online guests open the same panorama, worked out from the scene they
  already receive. The match server passes the option through as well.

## Street View, cleaner

Street View now only ever opens Google's own imagery. It used to fall back to
visitors' uploaded panoramas, which can be anything: a bird, a room, a selfie.
If Google refuses the key, the game stops dealing Street View-only places for
the rest of the session.
