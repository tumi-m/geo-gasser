# Ten rounds of fixes and polish

A phone-first audit (360–390 px touch, plus 1280 px desktop) of every screen:
home, tutorial, settings tabs, lobby, a live round, the reveal, the round
splashes and the final results. Each round below fixes what the audit found
and was checked in the browser before moving on.

1. **Modals lock the page behind them.** Every modal renders through a portal
   and marks the rest of the page `inert`, so Tab, screen readers and taps
   can't reach the game underneath. The state is restored on close.
2. **Comfortable tap targets.** The map zoom buttons are now 44 px on touch
   screens (Leaflet's own stylesheet had kept them at 30 px). Scene controls,
   colour swatches, switches, the volume slider and the map chips also get
   larger hit areas. The whole row of a settings toggle is tappable.
3. **The match HUD fits on phones.** At 360–390 px the Settings button used to
   fall off screen. Explore is now icon-only there, the solo score sits in one
   pill with the avatar, the duel tally drops the names and the timer shrinks.
   Below 375 px the score label hides too.
4. **The Atmosphere slider does something.** A soft ambient bed plays under
   the round when no music is on: wind with slow gusts and a quiet pad. It
   fades in and out and follows the Atmosphere volume.
5. **The reveal never hides the answer.** Long place names no longer push the
   country out of view. The map frames the answer clear of the card and dims
   its own labels. The pin label sits on a dark pill so it reads over any map.
   The round totals roll at the right moment in solo.
6. **The map fills wide panels.** The ocean grid now runs past the date line,
   so wide desktop maps no longer show empty bands.
7. **Settings map choices stop clipping names.** Map presets use two columns
   on phones, so "South Africa" and "SA × NL" show in full.
8. **Lobby.**
   - It shows one waiting status: an empty seat with a pinging ring. It used
     to say "Waiting for opponent" twice.
   - The Start button now reads "Start when they join".
   - Phones get a native **Share invite** button (the system share sheet).
   - Copying works even where the async clipboard is refused, and screen
     readers announce it.
9. **Shareable results.**
   - **Share** on the results screen sends a spoiler-free card with one
     coloured square per question, the score line (both names in a duel) and
     a link back. It uses the share sheet or the clipboard (`share-card.ts`,
     with tests).
   - The "Fast accurate" tile only counted guesses within 5 km, so it
     usually showed "—". It is replaced by **Best question**.
   - On phones the result buttons had collapsed to about 24 px tall
     (`flex-1` in a column); they are now full-height.
10. **Lighter match download.** three.js, the panorama viewer and the
    reconstruction scene load only when a round needs them. The match chunk
    shrinks from 1,041 KB to 505 KB; flat-photo rounds never fetch the 3D
    code.

## Validation

- 195 game tests pass; app and worker typecheck, lint and the production
  build are clean.
- Browser checks at 360, 390 and 768 px for each round. A full solo match
  was played to the results screen (with Share tested) after the lazy-loading
  change. No console errors.
- The panorama viewer couldn't be exercised live here (it needs Mapillary
  imagery). The build shows it split into its own chunk.
