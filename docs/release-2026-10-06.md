# The cosmos round: thirty places in our universe

Voyage and Odyssey now leave Earth. Voyage plays one cosmos round and Odyssey
plays two. Each cosmos round has ten questions drawn from thirty targets. The
shorter matches (Escape, Quick and Classic) never visit space.

## The targets

`src/lib/game/cosmos.ts`:

- **Planets:** Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune.
- **Moons:** the Moon, Io, Europa, Titan, Enceladus, Triton.
- **Dwarf worlds and small bodies:** Ceres, Pluto, Eris, Arrokoth, Halley's
  Comet.
- **The edge of the Solar System:** Voyager 1, the Oort cloud.
- **Stars:** Proxima Centauri, Sirius, Betelgeuse, the Pleiades.
- **The Milky Way:** the Orion Nebula, the Crab Nebula, Sagittarius A*.
- **Other galaxies:** the Large Magellanic Cloud, Andromeda.

## How a question plays

- **The scene is a live motion graphic, not a photo.** Every target is drawn
  live on a canvas:
  - Planets and moons are lit, textured spheres you can drag to turn.
    - Earth's continents come from the land dots we already ship.
    - Gas giants have swirling bands, and Jupiter has its Great Red Spot.
    - Saturn and Uranus have rings that pass in front of and behind the
      planet.
    - Io has volcanoes, Europa has cracked ice, Pluto has its heart and
      Enceladus has its tiger stripes.
    - Moons hang in front of their planet: Jupiter, Saturn, Neptune, or Earth
      for the Moon.
  - Stars have coronas and slow rays. Proxima flares, and Sirius has its
    small companion.
  - The comet has a dust tail and an ion tail. Voyager 1 sends radio pulses
    home. The Oort cloud turns in 3D around a Sun that is only a bright star.
  - Galaxies are thousands of stars turning at their own speeds. The Crab
    Nebula's pulsar flickers, and stars whip round the black hole.
- **Field notes.** One observation per target, such as "an iron-oxide dust
  world with two small moons". It never names the target.
- **Arrival.** Stars streak past for a second as you arrive.
- **Looking around.** Drag to turn, and scroll or pinch to zoom. The arrow
  keys and +/− work too.
- **The guess: how far from the Sun is this?** You place your guess on a
  ruler at the bottom, which uses a log scale running from inside Mercury's
  orbit to past Andromeda.
  - The ruler carries orbits with their planets, the asteroid and Kuiper
    belts, the haze of the Oort cloud, nearby stars, the Milky Way and
    distant galaxies. None of them is labelled; only the neighbourhoods
    have names.
  - Tap to place your guess, drag your pin to adjust, and zoom for a finer
    read.
  - With the keyboard, the arrows move your guess (shift for bigger steps)
    and Enter locks it.
- **Scoring.** A miss is measured in decades of distance (powers of ten).
  - Mistaking Jupiter for Saturn keeps about 40% of the accuracy points.
  - A miss by a factor of ten scores almost nothing.
  - The speed bonus works as it does on the map.
  - The reveal frames your pin, your opponent's pin and the answer, and
    states the gap in AU or light-years.
- **Grok** plays the cosmos as well. Now and then it takes a target for
  something else entirely.
- **Round intro.** Cosmos questions open on an orrery: eight planets on
  tilted orbits around the Sun.

## Kept fair and tidy

- **Map size.** On a small map the cosmos never outweighs the photos: a
  cosmos round is only dealt when the map fills at least as many rounds.
- **No answers sent early.** Guests receive only how to draw the target and
  its field note, never its name or distance.
- **Stats and results.**
  - Cosmos questions stay out of km statistics, the expedition route, the
    passport stamps and the "closest" line on the share card.
  - Their misses are shown in AU or light-years.
- **Loading.** The cosmos code loads only when a cosmos question appears,
  about 9 KB gzipped.

## Checks

- **Tests.** 219 tests pass, 11 of them new. They check:
  - thirty unique targets inside the ruler;
  - that no field note names its target;
  - deck slots: Voyage plays round 6 and Odyssey plays rounds 4 and 9, with
    reconstructions still closing the match;
  - that unseen targets are dealt first;
  - scoring, and that answers stay hidden;
  - Grok's guesses.
- **Builds.** App and worker typecheck, lint and the production build are
  clean.
- **Browser.** All thirty targets were rendered and reviewed at 1,280 px.
  Also checked:
  - the whole question on a phone: intro, scene, pin, lock and reveal;
  - a bot duel on desktop;
  - keyboard play with reduced motion;
  - that photo and reconstruction questions still use the map.
