# Three rounds of quality checks

Each round played the game the way people do: end to end, at phone and
desktop sizes, by touch, mouse and keyboard. What it found was then fixed.

## Round 1: every mode, start to finish

I played solo, a duel against Grok, pass and play, Quick mode, and rematches.
- **Grok kept you waiting.** It thought for 6 to 22 seconds whatever you
  did, so locking in fast meant watching "Grok is guessing…" for up to 20
  seconds. Once you lock in, Grok now answers within a beat.
  - Its score still uses the time it planned to take, so playing fast
    doesn't make it stronger.
  - Only the local bot can carry a planned time. Online locks never do, and
    a planned time can never be shorter than the real one (tested).
  - A full duel went from 113 automated steps to 37.
- **The duel reveal hid Grok's result on phones.** The fixes:
  - The map is a little shorter during the reveal.
  - The verdict headline folds to one line, and gives way entirely on short
    phones.
  - The speed tip steps aside in duels.
  - On iPhone SE-sized screens, Continue sits beside the title.
  - Both rows now fit at 360×740, 375×667, 390×844 and 430×932.
- **Losing read "RUNNER UP — Grok"**, as if Grok had come second. The results
  screen now reads "Champion" above the winner, with "Grok won by N points".
- **Stale "in" badges** are gone from the final tally.

## Round 2: settings, keyboard, accessibility, landscape

- **Keyboard play end to end.** "/" opens city search. On the focused map a
  crosshair appears, the arrow keys pan, Enter drops the pin at the centre
  and Enter again locks it.
- **Name prompt focus.** It focused the first bot shape, so typing went
  nowhere. With a mouse it now focuses the name field. On touch it starts at
  the top, so the keyboard doesn't cover the dialog.
- **Focus rings.** Text fields had no visible focus; they now show an
  accent ring.
- **axe-core scans** of nine screens found:
  - round pips labelled without a role
  - a score tally that claimed to be a table with no cells
  - a paragraph inside a list
  - a round screen with no heading

  All nine screens now scan clean.
- **Landscape phones.** The reveal was squeezed into 180 px; the card and map
  now sit side by side.
- **Copy.** "1 places" now reads "1 place", and very small custom maps say
  their matches will be short.

## Round 3: edge cases and performance

- **Edge cases that already held up:**
  - a reload mid-round resumes
  - a blocked photo offers Retry
  - a double-tapped Lock registers once
  - a round that times out with no pin reveals "No pin"
  - a rematch deals fresh places
- **Performance fix.** The round clock re-rendered the whole match screen 60
  times a second. It now ticks once a second, and the timer ring glides
  between seconds in CSS. The photo explorer also stops rewriting an
  unchanged transform every frame.
  - Measured with the CPU slowed 4×: the main thread is now 84% idle during
    a round, up from 41%.

## Checks

- 208 tests pass; app and worker typecheck, lint and the production build
  are clean.
- Three full matches (solo, bot duel, pass and play) reached their results.
- The 16-screen audit at 360, 390 and 1,280 px is clean.
