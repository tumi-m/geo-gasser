# Six rounds: fairer scoring, simpler layouts, more immersion

A fresh audit of every screen at 360, 390 and 1280 px, then six rounds of
changes. Each was checked in the browser at phone and desktop sizes.

## 1. Speed only pays when you're close (scoring bug)

A pin dropped anywhere in the first second earned about 10,000 of 20,000
points: a guess on the wrong continent scored like a careful, close one. The
speed bonus now scales with accuracy (`speedBonus` in `scoring.ts`).

- A perfect instant pin still earns 20,000.
- A far miss earns nothing for speed.
- The reveal labels the parts **Accuracy** and **Speed** and explains a lost
  bonus.
- The tutorial describes the rule.

## 2. The guessing map, rethought

The map used to carry a search field, three region chips, a pin chip, expand,
zoom, a scale bar, the Lock button and the pin readout all on top of itself.
On phones that covered about a third of it.

- **Tool strip above the map.** A region switch shows where you are; search,
  zoom-to-pin and expand are icon buttons.
- **One action bar below the map.** Where your pin landed, plus **Lock guess**.
  Before you've dropped a pin it says "Tap the map to drop a pin".
- **Removed:** the scale bar and the floating hint over the photo.
- **Desktop:** the docked map rests small over the photo and grows when you
  reach for it.
- **Bug fixed:** the desktop reveal stayed at world zoom. It framed the pins
  while the map was still widening from the corner. It now re-frames once the
  size settles, and after expanding.
- **Narrow phones:** the tool strip fits from 320 px up.

## 3. The Duel setup, rethought

On phones the full bot builder filled the first screen and pushed online play
below the fold.

- Your name and bot now share one row, with the builder behind **Edit** (it
  slides open).
- The three ways to play are cards: Duel Grok, Pass and play (player two
  opens in place), and Play online (create or join on one line).
- The whole screen fits a 360 px phone. Two slow orbits drift behind it.

## 4. Immersive rounds

- A vignette and moving film grain sit over the photo.
- In the last ten seconds a red edge closes in on every heartbeat, stronger as
  time runs out.
- Locking in blinks a camera shutter over the stage.
- At the reveal a glowing comet leads each arc to the answer, and yours lands
  with an impact burst.

## 5. Motion across the journey

- **Results open with your expedition:** a small map framed on the places the
  match visited.
  - The route draws itself hop by hop.
  - Numbered stops land in the colour of how you scored.
  - The geometry is tested in `src/lib/map/route.ts`.
- **Home, with a mouse:** the hero photo drifts against the pointer while the
  words move with it. Map cards shift their photo and catch a moving
  highlight.
- **Home, on phones:** card photos drift as they scroll past, on browsers that
  support scroll-driven animation.
- **Bugs fixed:**
  - Card hover lift works again; the scroll-in style had overridden it.
  - Long distances get thousands separators ("31,625 km").

## 6. Sweep and checks

- A full audit at 360, 390 and 1280 px found no overflow, no unlabelled
  controls and no console errors.
- 202 game tests pass; app and worker typecheck, lint and the production
  build are clean.
- All motion uses transform and opacity only. With reduced motion there is no
  grain, shutter or comet; the vignette and heartbeat stay, but still.
