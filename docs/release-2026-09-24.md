# End-of-round animation

The round verdict added yesterday played inside the result card while the
player was watching the map fly to the answer, and was over in about a
second — easy to miss entirely. The end of a round is now its own moment.

## Round splash (`src/components/game/round-splash.tsx`)

Over the photo, with the reveal map still visible below:

1. The round label drops in; both players slide in from either side, "vs"
   between them, names given by the players.
2. Each player's points for the round count up together.
3. The winner is crowned: their avatar grows with a spring, an accent ring
   pulses out twice, a trophy drops onto it, sparks burst, and the loser
   dims. "Tumi takes the round" rises in word by word, with who leads the
   match and by how much. A short two-note chime for a win, a softer one for
   a loss.
4. After about 2.5s it fades and the result card slides up.

Solo rounds get the same moment: the points count up large, the verdict
("So close", "Tough one") rises in, the distance underneath, and sparks for
a strong guess. Tap, or any key, skips it; the key does not also continue
the match.

## Also animated

- The result card now appears after the splash, so its own animation plays
  while the player is looking at it: the rows slide in from either side, the
  bars race, the headline pops, and a shimmer sweeps across the winner's row.
- The solo Score box rolls up to the new total and bumps as it lands.
- Final results: the score counts up from zero and sparks burst behind the
  winner's avatar.

Everything animates transform and opacity only (see the 2026-09-23
results-screen blur fix for why filters are avoided).

## Reduced motion

If the phone's Reduce Motion setting (or Settings → Reduce motion in the
game) is on, every animation is switched off. The splash still appears —
winner named at once, no sparks — and moves on after 1.8s, so the result is
never skipped silently.
