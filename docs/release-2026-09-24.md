# End-of-round animation

The round verdict added yesterday played inside the result card while the
player was watching the map fly to the answer, and was over in about a
second — easy to miss entirely. The end of a round is now its own moment.

## Round splash (`src/components/game/round-splash.tsx`)

Over the photo, with the reveal map still visible below:

1. The round label drops in; both players slide in from either side, "vs"
   between them, names given by the players.
2. Each player's points for the round count up together.
3. The winner is crowned: their avatar grows with a spring, an accent ring
   pulses out twice, a trophy drops onto it, sparks burst, and the loser
   dims. "Tumi takes the round" rises in word by word, with who leads the
   match and by how much. A short two-note chime for a win, a softer one for
   a loss.
4. After about 2.5s it fades and the result card slides up.

Solo rounds get the same moment: the points count up large, the verdict
("So close", "Tough one") rises in, the distance underneath, and sparks for
a strong guess. Tap, or any key, skips it; the key does not also continue
the match.

## Also animated

- The result card now appears after the splash, so its own animation plays
  while the player is looking at it: the rows slide in from either side, the
  bars race, the headline pops, and a shimmer sweeps across the winner's row.
- The solo Score box rolls up to the new total and bumps as it lands.
- Final results: the score counts up from zero and sparks burst behind the
  winner's avatar.

Everything animates transform and opacity only (see the 2026-09-23
results-screen blur fix for why filters are avoided).

## Reduced motion

If the phone's Reduce Motion setting (or Settings → Reduce motion in the
game) is on, every animation is switched off. The splash still appears —
winner named at once, no sparks — and moves on after 1.8s, so the result is
never skipped silently.

## Verification

Browser at 390 × 844, against Grok as "Tumi": the card waits for the splash;
names, trophy and sparks on the winner; the headline; the splash leaves on
its own; Enter skips it without continuing the match; a tap skips it. Solo:
points shown, the final score counts up (566 → 48,189), the burst renders.
Reduced motion: the splash still names the winner, no sparks, then the card.

# Round summary for Quick and longer modes

Quick mode is one round of ten questions, so "the end of the round" is after
question 10 — and nothing marked it: every question got the same splash
(labelled "Q3/10"), then the game went straight to the final results. The
same gap sat at the end of every 10-question round in Standard and longer.

## Fixed

- **Round summary** (`round-summary-splash.tsx`, logic in
  `src/lib/game/round-summary.ts` with five tests). After the last question
  of a round, following that question's splash:
  - duel: both players with the round total rolling up, a row of ten pips
    lighting in turn in the colour of whoever took each question (yours lime,
    theirs orange, ties grey), then the round's winner crowned — "Grok wins
    the round · 7 of 10 questions · by 14,409";
  - solo: the round total, a bar per question rising in turn with your best
    one lit, then a rating ("Great round", "Solid round") and your best
    location with its score.
- Per-question splashes in multi-question rounds say "Tumi takes question 3",
  not "takes the round".
- **Reduced motion no longer means no animation.** With the phone's Reduce
  Motion on, every animation used to be cut to nothing, so the splash was a
  still frame. The splashes now keep the same beats and trade movement for
  cross-fades; the numbers still roll. Sparks stay off.
- A key held down, or pressed within 350ms of a splash appearing, no longer
  skips it (a player locking with Enter as the reveal lands).
- **Hydration failure on the home and duel pages** for anyone with saved
  settings — every returning player. Both read the stored name and settings
  during the first render, so the server's HTML ("Traveler") never matched
  the client's, and React threw the page away and rebuilt it. They are now
  read after mount. Checked on /, /duel, /play, /duel/bot and /duel/hotseat
  with a saved name: all clean.

## Verification

Full Quick matches played in the browser at 390 × 844: against Grok (a
splash after all ten questions, the summary with ten pips and the winner,
then the result card and final results), solo, and solo with reduced motion
(bars fading in, total rolling, "Solid round"). The 5-round splash suite
re-run: all pass.
